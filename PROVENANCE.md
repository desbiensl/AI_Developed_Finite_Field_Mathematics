# Research provenance and attribution

## Origins and roles

The five documents record a mathematical investigation initiated and directed by **Louis Desbiens**, who supplied the research questions and goals, guided the project conceptually, selected the material to make publicly available and curated its release.

**OpenAI's ChatGPT** contributed extensively to the mathematical material, including exploring approaches, algebraic derivations, candidate theorems and arguments, computational checks, attempts to identify exceptions or logical gaps, literature organization, and drafting and revision of the manuscripts. These are mathematical and research contributions, not merely copyediting.

Louis Desbiens is the **human initiator and curator**, not the guarantor that every advanced result is correct. ChatGPT is the **AI mathematical research contributor**, not a conventional scholarly coauthor. No independent mathematical verifier is identified or implied.

## Evidence and confidence

The manuscripts contain (a) mathematical background from cited public literature, (b) proposed deductions and proofs developed in this investigation, and (c) selected finite or symbolic checks, including test programs reproduced here. These are different evidentiary categories. A computational match on a finite sample does not establish a universal theorem. A proof written by an AI and reviewed by the same AI is not independently reviewed. No formal proof-assistant certification has been completed.

The supplied machine-checkable certificates concern specific numerical values and finite computations; they do not independently certify the large theoretical claims. Complete originality and priority checks have not been performed. See `VERIFICATION.md` for per-manuscript details.

## Publication intent

The collection is publicly offered as *mathematical research artifacts pending independent verification* to invite independent examination, critical review, corrections and further development. It is not submitted here as a peer-reviewed article, and is not represented as a validated HAL/arXiv preprint. Future independent reviewers may validate, qualify or refute any specific statement. A later traditional article would need an accountable author and adequate independent assessment.

## Institutional independence and contact

No institution, employer or research group is claimed as affiliated with or endorsing this material. The work is described by the curator as independently undertaken without institutional resources or funding. No technical-correspondence obligation or personal email address is attached to the manuscripts. Attribution under the curator's real name does not confer anonymity.

## Responsibilities

The curator is responsible for deciding to publish, giving a good-faith account of the research process, disclosing AI involvement, complying with applicable intellectual-property obligations, and responding appropriately if a material issue is recognized. The curator does **not** assert personal mathematical certification of the proposed results. This distinction is not a legal waiver of ordinary obligations.
