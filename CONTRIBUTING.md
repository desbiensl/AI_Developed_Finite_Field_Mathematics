# How to contribute independent mathematical review

This is a public collection of AI-developed research candidates, **not** a certified mathematics publication. The human curator may not be available to answer specialized correspondence or maintain the repository continuously.

Useful contributions include: a self-contained correction to a specific equation or proof step; a counterexample with precise hypotheses and reproducible calculations; a missing published reference; an independent proof; or a formally checked theorem with its complete axiom audit. Identify the article number and exact statement, state the original hypotheses, and distinguish a true counterexample from an exceptional case omitted in a test script.

For proposed geometric and Fourier results, please identify precisely the published theorem being invoked and verify its hypotheses, including character sheaves, descent, weights, irreducibility and connectedness. Sampled finite-field agreement by itself is not sufficient for this purpose.

Independent reviews should state the scope checked, the reviewer's mathematical assumptions and the status of any unresolved dependencies. The curator cannot promise technical support or immediate integration of a correction.
