# How to cite or acknowledge the collection

The five manuscripts in this repository are **unverified mathematical
research artifacts**, not peer-reviewed papers or independently certified
theorems. Please cite the specific manuscript and version used.

Suggested collection citation:

> *Explorations in finite-field mathematics: AI-developed research artifacts
> pending independent verification*, edition of 31 October 2026. Human
> initiator and curator: Louis Desbiens; AI mathematical research contributor:
> OpenAI's ChatGPT. Public GitHub collection (repository URL to be supplied
> after publication). CC BY 4.0.

If referencing a result, identify the article, claim number and the statement's
verification status. A successful finite test is not a proof of a general
claim. Attribution does not imply Louis Desbiens or OpenAI endorses a changed
version. Use [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) for limitations and review
priorities and [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for reuse conditions.
